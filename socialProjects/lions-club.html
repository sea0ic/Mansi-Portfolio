Creative Advocacy

SIMA Across Borders

As part of our efforts to introduce complex global issues in an accessible way, this program used film as a medium to engage children in conversations around climate change. In collaboration with SIMA Academy, we screened The Last Ice Age across two sessions, followed by guided discussions that helped students better understand environmental challenges and their role in protecting the planet.

Lions English Connect

Lions English Connect was a community outreach program where we worked with local NGOs to help Indonesian children build confidence in speaking English. With volunteers from multiple countries, the sessions focused on conversational English and real-life communication, giving students the opportunity to practice with native and fluent speakers in a supportive environment.

Gift Of Sight

The Gift of Sight was a collaborative initiative with multiple Lions Clubs in Surabaya and abroad, focused on improving children's access to eye care. Through this project, we provided over 1,000 free eye check-ups and eyeglasses to children in Surabaya, helping support their health, learning, and overall well-being.

Saturday Happy & Healthy

Saturday Happy & Healthy was a collaborative health initiative with Lions Club Patria Youth, aimed at promoting early health awareness in the community. The program provided free medical screenings for diabetes, cholesterol, and uric acid, along with basic educational sessions for children focused on hygiene and healthy daily habits.
