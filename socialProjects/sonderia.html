The Story Behind Sonderia

Sonderia comes from the word “sonder”—the realization that every person around us is living a life just as complex and meaningful as our own.
I created Sonderia with the vision of helping children understand this idea early on: that every person and every culture deserves appreciation and respect. The project was proposed during the 9th Youth Leadership Workshop, where it received a UNESCO grant, which supported the project financially.
Through Sonderia, we worked on two main goals—creating an animated film that teaches children about cultural diversity and inclusivity, and hosting a two-day virtual cultural exchange that brought together kids from different countries, cultures, and backgrounds.
