ER Model & Schema

11 core entities with proper relationships, cardinality constraints, and disjoint employee specialization (Cashier, Chef, Waiter, Manager).

⭐

Loyalty System

Star-based rewards with automatic discount tiers (10★=5%, 25★=10%, 50★=15%, 100★=20%). Audit trail for all star transactions.

🔄

Order Workflow

Complete lifecycle from PENDING → QUEUED → IN_PROCESS → COMPLETED → DELIVERED with 30-minute SLA tracking and alerts.

💰

Payment Processing

Multi-method payment (Cash/Credit/Debit) with PCI-compliant storage, refund management requiring manager approval, and financial audit trail.
