01 — The Problem Discovery

When I downloaded IndiaMart, I instantly knew why users don't trust it

I couldn't ignore the elephant in the room. IndiaMart has a 1.6/5 star rating. Not 3.5. Not even 2.5. 1.6 out of 5.

For a company that's been in business for over 30 years, serving 8.6 million suppliers and 130 million buyers, this isn't just bad — it's a crisis. And if not addressed properly, IndiaMart risks losing the one thing no amount of marketing can buy back: credibility.

So I did what any PM would do — I downloaded the app myself.

Within 5 minutes of searching for a simple product, my phone exploded with calls and messages from dozens of sellers. No filtering. No vetting. Just chaos. That's when I understood viscerally why buyers don't trust this platform.

Meanwhile, Alibaba Group, IndiaMART’s largest global competitor, sits at a 3.8/5 rating and is increasingly leveraging AI to improve trust signals and buyer experience.

Which made me wonder:
If platforms like Alibaba are already using AI to strengthen trust and user experience, could a similar AI-driven approach help solve IndiaMART’s trust problem?

Because the real issue here isn’t the lack of new features. IndiaMART doesn’t necessarily need another flashy product addition — it needs to solve the trust crisis at the core of the platform first. If buyers feel safe using the platform, everything else improves naturally: engagement, repeat usage, and ultimately revenue.

02 — The Strategic Bet

BuyerShield AI: a trust layer built into IndiaMart

BuyerShield is an LLM-powered trust layer embedded directly into IndiaMart's existing experience. It shows up automatically at three critical moments:

1

Search Results Page

A trust badge appears next to every seller listing — green, amber, or red - before a buyer even opens a profile. High-risk sellers get flagged instantly.

2

Seller Profile Page

A BuyerShield panel automatically appears showing GST verification status, complaint history, and a plain-language risk assessment. No manual checking needed.

3

Live Chat Window

Real-time alerts flag suspicious behavior — WhatsApp redirects, pressure tactics, payment traps - before the buyer makes a mistake.

The system works by pulling data from government APIs (GST portal, MCA registry), IndiaMart's own complaint database, and behavioral patterns from 20 years of transactions. An LLM processes all of this in under 5 seconds and translates it into a single, clear answer: safe, caution, or risk.

What usually takes a buyer 2–3 hours of manual verification can be done within seconds and is more accurate because it sees patterns individual buyers never could.

03 — Why This Works

The features that actually move the needle

Not every feature matters equally. I used RICE scoring (Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort) to prioritize ruthlessly. Here's what made the cut for the 6-month build:

🟢

GST/MCA Verification (RICE: 108)

Instantly checks if a seller is a real registered business. Widest reach, highest impact, fastest to build. This is the foundation.

🟢

Complaint History Pull (RICE: 95.6)

Surfaces past disputes and resolution patterns. Uses IndiaMart's own data — zero external dependencies. Clearest predictor of future fraud.

🟠

WhatsApp Redirect Alert (RICE: 45)

Catches the single most common scam gateway on IndiaMart — sellers asking buyers to move conversations off-platform.

🟠

Live Chat Detection (RICE: 17.9)

Monitors for pressure tactics, urgency language, and payment traps. This is the most complex to build, but catches what the other features miss.

The first two features ship in Month 3. The second two in Month 6. Document forgery detection (invoices, fake certificates) gets parked for V3 — it's the most technically complex and needs V1 and V2 data to train properly.

04 — The Hard Truths

Limitations of the LLM — and how we can handle it

BuyerShield may sometimes flag legitimate or new sellers as risky, and outages on Indian government sites can affect results.

So I designed the system to be honest about its uncertainty:

📊

Confidence Indicators

Every score shows its confidence level: High (85%+), Medium (60–84%), or Low (<60%). If data is stale or incomplete, the buyer sees it.

🔄

Human Review Option

Buyers can challenge any assessment. Simple cases trigger automatic re-checks. Complex cases go to IndiaMart's trust team for manual review.

🎯

Feedback Loop

Every transaction outcome feeds back into the model. Did a 92-score seller deliver? Did a 34-score seller turn out legitimate? The system learns continuously.

06 — The Business Case

Why IndiaMart should build this

IndiaMART has ₹1,550 crore in annual revenue. This solution is about protecting that revenue from the churn crisis.

Here's the chain reaction that's already happening:

Buyers get scammed → Buyers leave platform → Sellers get fewer quality leads → Sellers see no ROI → Sellers cancel subscriptions → Revenue drops.

BuyerShield breaks that chain at the first link. More trusted buyers means better lead quality, which means higher seller retention, which means IndiaMart's subscription model holds.

The Kicker

IndiaMart has the scale, the cash reserves (₹2,874 crore), and the market position. The only thing not growing fast enough is trust. BuyerShield fixes that — and it compounds every other advantage IndiaMart already has.

07 — What I Learned

The PM lessons from this case study

1

Use the product yourself. I probably wouldn’t have understood the spam problem just from reading reviews. Downloading IndiaMart and using it myself helped me see what buyers actually experience.

2

Build around what the product already has. Instead of imagining completely new businesses, I tried to think about how IndiaMart could use something it already has — years of transactional and behavioral data.

3

Think about uncertainty, not just ideal scenarios. While working on the Trust & Transparency section, I realized systems should acknowledge when data is limited instead of appearing overly certain.

4

Prioritization matters. There were many ideas that sounded interesting, but for a first version I tried focusing on a smaller set of features that felt more practical to start with.

5

Explaining the problem clearly is important. While building this portfolio page, I realized that presenting the reasoning behind decisions matters almost as much as the solution itself.
