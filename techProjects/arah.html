ARAH

A comprehensive scholarship tracking platform helping students discover, manage, and apply to opportunities that match their profile.

Project Overview

ARAH (meaning "direction" in Indonesian) was created to solve a critical problem: students missing out on scholarships simply because they didn't know they existed. Many opportunities go unfilled because information is scattered across different platforms, deadlines are hard to track, and requirements are unclear.

The app aggregates scholarship opportunities from multiple sources, uses smart filtering to match students with relevant programs, and sends personalized notifications to ensure no deadline is missed. It's like having a scholarship advisor in your pocket.

Built with a mobile-first approach, ARAH makes scholarship hunting accessible to students anywhere, anytime—transforming a frustrating, time-consuming process into a streamlined experience.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Data Aggregation

Scholarship information is scattered across hundreds of websites with inconsistent formats. Solution: Built custom web scrapers with Puppeteer to automatically collect and standardize data from multiple sources.

Challenge: Matching Algorithm

Creating accurate recommendations without overwhelming users. Solution: Implemented a weighted scoring system considering GPA, interests, location preferences, and past application behavior.

Challenge: User Retention

Keeping students engaged between application cycles. Solution: Added features like scholarship prep resources, essay tips, and a community forum for peer support.

Challenge: Data Privacy

Handling sensitive student information securely. Solution: Implemented end-to-end encryption for documents, GDPR-compliant data handling, and transparent privacy controls.
